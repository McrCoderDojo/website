Equipment

If you have access to a laptop, Macbook, please try to bring that.

We have a small number of spare laptops and Raspberry Pis available if you don’t have your own.

Software

Scratch

Scratch is a graphical programming language where you can create your own interactive stories, games, and animations.
You can download offline version or use Scratch online .

Python

Python is a general purpose programming language. Hence, you can use the programming language for developing both desktop and web applications. Also, you can use Python for developing complex scientific and numeric applications.

You can down it at https://www.python.org/downloads/

Food

There is a kitchen at Ada for making drinks. There are numerous shops and food outlets near the building.

Y ou are welcome to bring your own food and drinks.

Important

If you are 13 or under, then please make sure a parent or guardian is with you throughout the event.

If you’re over 13, then we will ask for details of an emergency contact person.

Please make sure you take care of your valuables at all times.
