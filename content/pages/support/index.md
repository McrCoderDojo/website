We rely on our brilliant volunteers and supporters to make Manchester CoderDojo happen.

We receive no funding and don’t charge anyone to take part.

Want to support us? Here’s how:

- Come along and take part!
- Get involved in **[STEM and volunteer](https://www.stem.org.uk/stem-ambassadors)**
- **Donate** – We are in the process of looking for funding to upgrade our laptops
